# 📱 Quick Start: Install Your App

## ✅ Your App is Ready!

Your app now works like a **native app** on iOS and Android!

---

## 🚀 How to Install (For Users)

### **On Android:**
1. Open `https://depopmoldova.vercel.app` in Chrome
2. Tap "Install" when the prompt appears
3. App icon appears on home screen
4. Done! ✅

### **On iOS:**
1. Open `https://depopmoldova.vercel.app` in Safari
2. Tap Share button (⬆️)
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. Done! ✅
